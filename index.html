How we make a paper pencil

Eight easy steps, from old paper to your hand.

  1. Cut the paper

    Old newspaper or industrial waste paper is cut into strips.

  2. Apply glue

    Our own glue goes on each strip. It leaves very little residue once dry.

  3. Feed the machine

    The machine rolls the paper around the graphite lead.

  4. Dry in the sun

    Pencils dry in the sun, then are cut to the same length.

  5. Polish

    A polishing machine makes the body smooth.

  6. Add colour

    We paint with FSSAI-approved food colours.

  7. Fit the seed capsule

    Only for seed pencils. A small seed capsule is fixed on the end.

  8. Pack and send

    Pencils go into a strong recycled box and are sent to you.

Why we started making paper pencils

Our founder has always cared about the environment. He wanted a business that helps nature.

Sivakasi has a big printing industry, so paper is easy to find. That gave him the idea to recycle paper into pencils.

In 2013 we started designing our own machines and glue. Today we also supply machinery and raw materials.

Questions about our pencils

Quick answers about the pencil, how it is made, and seed pencils.

About the pencil

Is a paper pencil strong?

Yes. The paper is rolled tightly with glue around the graphite lead, so the pencil is strong and sharpens with a normal sharpener.

Are the colours safe for children?

We use only food colours approved by FSSAI. Pencils are not meant to be chewed, but we take extra care because children sometimes chew them.

How it is made

Is the pencil really made without wood?

Yes. No wood and no trees are used. Recycled paper is rolled around the lead.

What glue do you use?

We use a synthetic glue that we formulated ourselves. It leaves very little residue once dry.

What paper do you use?

Old newspaper from collection points, or white paper from industrial waste.

Seed pencils and orders

How do I plant a seed pencil?

Use the pencil until it is short. Then plant the seed capsule end in soil and water it.

Can I get my logo printed?

Yes. We print logos and messages on pencils. Send us your design, quantity and delivery country for price and lead time.

Need paper pencils for your school or brand?

Tell us the product and quantity. We reply on WhatsApp or phone.
