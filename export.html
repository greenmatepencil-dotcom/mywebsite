What to tell us in your first enquiry

The more of this you send, the faster and more accurate our quotation will be.

For pencils and pens

Product type, quantity, pencil colour, logo/artwork, packing you want, delivery city and port, and your preferred delivery date.

For machines

Your country, electricity supply (220V single-phase or 415V three-phase), target pieces per hour/day, floor area, and whether you need semi-auto or automatic feeding.

For both

Your company name, website or social page if you have one, and whether you want FOB or CIF pricing. We will confirm what is possible after reviewing the details.

How the order works

  1. Enquiry

    Send your requirements on WhatsApp or email, or use the enquiry form with your country and quantity.

  2. Proposal

    We reply with the product or machine configuration, available options and a quotation. Prices, lead time and payment terms are confirmed in writing.

  3. Sample or factory test

    For pencils we can discuss samples. For machines we factory-test before dispatch and can share a running video.

  4. Packing & dispatch

    Pencils are packed in recycled boxes. Machines are packed in wooden crates with manuals and spare blades, with optional raw materials.

Common questions from overseas buyers

Ordering

Is there a minimum order?

Small orders are welcome for standard pencils and pens. Custom printing and machine orders have their own quantity requirements, which we confirm in the quotation.

How are prices quoted?

We share product or machine pricing after we know your specification, quantity, packing and destination. We can discuss FOB or CIF depending on the order.

What are the payment terms?

Payment terms are part of the written quotation and depend on the order. We do not ask you to pay before you have received our confirmed proposal.

Machines

What power supply do the machines need?

Our standard machines run on 220V single-phase. We also build for 415V three-phase lines and discuss motors and wiring suitable for your country before confirming the order.

Can a semi-automatic machine be upgraded later?

Yes. We build semi-auto and automatic versions, and we can discuss upgrades such as hopper feeding and printing attachment when you specify your target output.

What support is included with a machine?

Every machine comes with a manual, spare blades and factory testing. We provide commissioning guidance and discuss on-site support separately for overseas orders.

Products & compliance

Are the colours safe for children?

We use food-grade colours approved by FSSAI in India. Tell us which market you are supplying and we will share the material and colour documents available for your review.

Can you print our logo?

Yes. Send your artwork, the product and the quantity. We will confirm printing options and pricing.

Do you supply raw materials with machines?

Yes. We can include our glue, paper reels and 2B graphite leads in the same consignment as your machines.

Ready to get a quotation?

Send your product, quantity and destination — or your machine requirement and power supply. We reply on WhatsApp or email.
